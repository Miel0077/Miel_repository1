Who we are

Curious about how places take shape.

We are a student-made website exploring architecture, landscape, and the spaces people use every day. The page brings together visual references and ideas about form, materials, and a sense of place. It shares an interest in architecture without presenting itself as a professional architecture practice.

Our visual direction takes inspiration from the supplied moodboard and its architectural imagery. Warm earth tones, green accents, and natural textures shape the page’s look. The content introduces architecture through these themes.

Design principles

Look closer.
Build with care.

A considered design begins by noticing what is already there, then making choices that feel connected to their surroundings.

01 / PLACE

Read the landscape

Consider the setting, the light, and the natural features that give a place its character.

02 / MATERIAL

Choose with intention

Let material, texture, and structure contribute to a clear and cohesive whole.

03 / LIVING

Make room for life

Shape spaces around the everyday rhythms and experiences they are meant to hold.

Quick science trivia

Did you know?

01

A day on Venus is longer than its year

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only about 225 Earth days to travel around the Sun.

02

Octopuses have three hearts

Two hearts move blood to the gills, while a third sends it around the rest of the body.

03

Some trees communicate underground

Tree roots and soil fungi can form networks that help move nutrients and chemical signals between plants.

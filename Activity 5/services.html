What we consider

Design, from the ground up.

A thoughtful project takes shape through connected decisions. These areas offer a way to begin the conversation.

01 / SITE & CONTEXT

Understanding the place

Look at the site, its landscape, light, approaches, and existing character to establish a meaningful starting point for design.

02 / CONCEPT & SPACE

Shaping the idea

Develop an overall direction for how spaces relate, how people move through them, and how the design responds to its setting.

03 / MATERIAL & LIGHT

Choosing what you experience

Consider surfaces, textures, openings, and daylight as part of the atmosphere and everyday use of a space.

04 / DETAIL & CONNECTION

Bringing parts together

Pay attention to transitions and the way elements meet, helping the larger idea feel coherent at a human scale.

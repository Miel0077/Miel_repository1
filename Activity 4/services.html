SPACE

Saturn would float—hypothetically!

Saturn's average density is lower than water's. There isn't an ocean big enough to hold it, but the comparison is still wild.

MATH

Some infinities are bigger than others

The whole numbers never end, but the real numbers cannot be listed in a sequence. Mathematicians call their sizes different infinities.

PHYSICS

Lightning can outheat the Sun's surface

A lightning channel can reach around 30,000 kelvins, while the Sun's visible surface is about 5,800 kelvins.

Science & math trivia

Prepare to do a double take.

Space01

A year shorter than a day

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to spin once, but only about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. One Venus day is longer than its year!

Animals02

Wombats make cube-shaped poop

Wombat intestines shape their droppings into cubes, which are less likely to roll away when used to mark territory.

Math03

0.999... is exactly 1

The repeating decimal 0.999... isn't almost one; it equals one. For example, one third is 0.333..., and three thirds make 0.999... = 1.

Ocean04

Sharks are older than trees

Shark ancestors appeared in the fossil record over 400 million years ago, before the first known true trees.

Numbers05

There are different sizes of infinity

There are infinitely many whole numbers, but mathematicians have shown that the real numbers form a larger infinity.

Space06

Neutron stars are ridiculously dense

A sugar-cube-sized amount of neutron-star material would have a mass of roughly a billion tons on Earth.
